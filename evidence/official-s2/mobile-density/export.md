# Your words deserve a home

A thought begins in a quiet place. A notebook. A margin. A line you return to.

Publishing should keep that sense of ownership intact.

## Start with the author

An identity gives a piece of writing a clear origin. A signature makes that intention visible.

> A permanent record can still leave room for a changing mind.

New ideas become new versions. The earlier words remain part of the story.
